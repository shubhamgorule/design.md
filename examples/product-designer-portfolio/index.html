About

How I work

Partners describe my process as structured but flexible—heavy on shared artifacts, light on surprises in critique.

I start from problems that matter to users and the business, then make the invisible visible: maps, flows, and prototypes that teams can react to early.

I care about accessible defaults, resilient components, and documentation that survives the next reorg.

Outside of product work, I mentor designers transitioning to IC leadership and occasionally write about tokens, critique culture, and shipping with taste.
